Version 1.1.5.

- Initial F-Droid release.
- The read-only view now keeps Markdown formatting while searching, and
  adds an outline, reading stats, per-note scroll position, keyboard
  paging and system text scaling. The floating outline appears only when
  the reading pane is wide enough; phones keep just the back-to-top
  button.
- Invalid LaTeX formulas show a localized error with a copy button.
- Completed English, Italian and French localization of the remaining
  desktop strings.
