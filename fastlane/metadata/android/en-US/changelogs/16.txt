Version 1.1.6.

- Reading, editing and focus mode now share the same per-note scroll position
  when switching between them.
- Focus mode preserves the editor state and selection while the app bar and
  side panels are hidden.
- Small screens no longer show floating reading controls that overlap the
  text column.
- On phones and tablets, the top bar keeps a Settings shortcut at the far
  right when no note is open, with the sync indicator immediately to its
  left.
